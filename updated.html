Featured article · Business Planning

How to Build a Business Plan That Actually Works

A business plan is more than a document for investors. It is a framework for testing whether an idea can become a sustainable business.

1. Start with the problem

Define the customer, the problem they face and why the problem is important enough to pay to solve.

2. Explain the value proposition

Show what your product does differently and why customers would choose it over existing alternatives.

3. Build the business model

Map revenue streams, pricing, costs and unit economics. Make it clear how one customer creates revenue and what it costs to serve them.

4. Plan the go-to-market

Choose realistic acquisition channels and explain the sales process. Prioritize strategies that can be tested quickly.

5. Make the numbers tell the story

Build simple three-year assumptions for revenue, costs, cash flow and break-even. Treat forecasts as hypotheses that need evidence.

6. Finish with risks and milestones

Identify key risks, define measurable KPIs and set the next 90-day milestones. Review the plan as new evidence arrives.

The takeaway: the strongest business plans are concise, assumption-driven and designed to evolve. Educational content only — not financial advice.
